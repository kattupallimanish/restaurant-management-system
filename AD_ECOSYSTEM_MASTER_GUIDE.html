QR Ad Ecosystem

Master Management & Configuration Guide

This guide explains how to control, activate, target, and rank/prioritize ads served on client phones directly from your Google Sheets dashboard.

1. Activating and Deactivating Ads

Inside the Google Sheet tab named "AdCampaigns", there is a dedicated column titled Status. You can instantly control whether an ad is eligible to serve by modifying this field:

  • Set to ACTIVE: The ad is enabled and will be considered for display based on user traffic and bids.
  • Set to INACTIVE: The ad is instantly disabled and will not show on any screens.

2. Setting Where Ads Display (Placements)

You can target an ad campaign to show on one or more screens by writing the appropriate keywords into the Placements column in the Google Sheet. You can specify multiple screens by separating them with a comma (e.g. menu_landing, order_tracking).

3. Ad Ranking & Prioritization (How Ads are Filtered)

To maximize advertising revenue and trust, ads are prioritized using an **eCPM (expected Cost Per Mille)** yield optimization score. The engine calculates priority using this formula:

eCPM = (Bid CPC * Historical CTR) * 1000

Where:

  • Bid CPC: The amount the advertiser pays per click (configured in the Bid CPC sheet column).
  • Historical CTR (Click-Through Rate): Calculated dynamically as Clicks / Impressions from the logged analytics.

💡 Optimization Rules:

  1. Higher Bids = Higher Rank: An ad with a higher CPC bid will display more frequently.
  2. Higher Engagement = Higher Rank: If users click an ad often (high CTR), the system ranks it higher since it provides more revenue per impression.
  3. Exploration Phase: Newly added ads are given a temporary engagement bonus to collect baseline stats before competing strictly on eCPM yield.

4. Best Practices

  • Ensure your Image URLs link directly to high-resolution, fast-loading images (Unsplash links or secure cloud links work great).
  • Write headlines that emphasize the **benefit** (e.g. 🎁 7 Day Free Trial) to capture higher CTR and rank.
